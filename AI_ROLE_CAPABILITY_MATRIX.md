## Parity snapshot

The registry now has no unexplained `MISSING` rows for the supported marketplace surface. Buyer cart/favorites/messages/reviews/disputes/support/profile flows, seller inventory/fitment/coupons/store/order/message/analytics flows, and admin on-behalf listing/store/account/moderation/support flows are implemented with server-side role checks and confirmation for writes. `PARTIAL` rows are deliberate boundaries: compatibility requires explicit vehicle data, checkout preview stops before grouped order creation, order lookup does not copy a full private timeline into model context, and private verification/evidence upload remains the secure dedicated UI. Permanent deletion/archive, infrastructure controls, and `سيارتي` are intentionally excluded.
